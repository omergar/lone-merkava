    HOW TO PLAY

    You command and lay the gun roughly. When it points near a target the gunner sight opens. Centre the cross with fine aim and fire. Range and elevation are automatic. A hit that does not kill halts the target for a few seconds. Rounds scatter more with distance: a sure hit at 300 m, about one in two at 2000 m.

    The ditch can be crossed only on a laid bridge or at the two ground passes, shown green on the map. Kill bridge layers and the columns must detour to a pass. Units that reach the base damage it.

    Firing ramps (sand squares on the map) lift you above the ground for long shots, but you are exposed on top. Stay spotted in one place and artillery finds you, so shoot and move. Infantry and commandos carry RPGs. Flooring the engine makes smoke that reveals you.
